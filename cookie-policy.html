1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how it's being used.

2. Cookies We Use

Necessary

Required for the site to function correctly (e.g. remembering your cookie preference itself). These cannot be switched off.

Analytics

Help us understand which pages are popular and how visitors move through the site, so we can improve it. Used only if you allow them.

Marketing

Used to show you more relevant offers about our design services across the web. Used only if you allow them.

3. Managing Your Preferences

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies at any time on our Cookie Settings page, or through your browser settings.
